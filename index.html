Welcome to Sheep Station.

The best pattern is 10 sheep per grazed acre and 10 kilos of grain per sheep. Sow 10 kilos per acre for a 100 kilo harvest per acre.

How to Play

  • Start with 1,000 sheep, 200 acres, $10,000 in the bank, and 10,000 kilos of grain. The station’s opening value is $50,000.
  • Each year, the bank first collects a $1,000 mortgage payment.
  • Then choose whether to buy or sell land, trade sheep for money or grain, graze land, feed sheep, and sow grain.
  • After resolving the year, the report shows sheep born and lost, grain harvested, market values, and total station value.
  • Use the Advice button for original-style hints. Use Quit to end and value the property.
